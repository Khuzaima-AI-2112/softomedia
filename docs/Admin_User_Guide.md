## 2. Training Guide: Location Owners (Screen Owners)

**Objective**: Teach location owners how to register their screens and ensure they are online.

### Step 1: Connecting a Screen
1.  Open the web browser on the Smart TV or Digital Signage Player.
2.  Navigate to the Player URL provided by the Admin.
    *   Format: `https://app.softomedia.net/?screen_id=UNIQUE_SCREEN_NAME`
    *   Example: `https://app.softomedia.net/?screen_id=lobby-tv-01`
3.  **Registration**: On the first load, the screen will automatically register itself with the SoftoMedia backend.
4.  **Verification**: You will see a "Status: Online & Registered" message on the screen.

### Step 2: Troubleshooting
*   **"Offline" Status**: Check the device's internet connection. Refresh the page.
*   **Black Screen**: Ensure the browser is active and not in "sleep" mode.

## 3. Training Guide: Advertisers

**Objective**: Teach advertisers how to view their campaign performance.

### Step 1: Logging In
1.  Go to `https://app.softomedia.net/login`.
2.  Enter the email and password provided by the SoftoMedia Admin.

### Step 2: Dashboard Navigation
*   **Campaigns**: View active ad campaigns.
*   **Analytics**: Check impression counts and spend.
    *   **Impressions**: Number of times your ad was shown.
    *   **Completion Rate**: Percentage of ads played to the end.

---

## 4. Operational Best Practices

*   **Screen IDs**: Use a consistent naming convention (e.g., `[location]-[room]-[number]`, like `nyc-lobby-01`).
*   **Security**: Never share Admin credentials. Create separate accounts for operational staff.
*   **Monitoring**: Check the Admin Dashboard daily for offline screens.
